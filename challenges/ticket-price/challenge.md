A cinema sets its prices by age:

| Age | Price |
|---|---|
| under 5 | free |
| 5 to 17 | 6 |
| 18 to 64 | 10 |
| 65 and over | 7 |

Your program is given a person's **age** (a whole number). Print the price as a number, or `free` for children under 5.

Pay attention to the edges: someone who is exactly 18 pays 10, not 6.
