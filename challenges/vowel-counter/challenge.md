---
title: Vowel Counter
difficulty: easy
topics: [strings, loops]
order: 7
---

Your program is given one line of text. Count how many **vowels** it contains and print the number.

The vowels are `a`, `e`, `i`, `o` and `u`, in either lowercase or uppercase. `y` doesn't count. Everything else (consonants, spaces, punctuation, digits) is ignored.

For example, `Hello World` has 3 vowels: `e`, `o` and `o`.
