Every line has the same shape; only the multiplier changes, from 1 to 10. Loop over the multipliers.

---

`range(1, 11)` gives `1, 2, ..., 10`, because `range` stops **before** its end value. An f-string can do maths inside the braces: `f"{n * i}"`.

---

`for i in range(1, 11):` then `print(f"{n} x {i} = {n * i}")`.
