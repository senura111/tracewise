---
title: Times Table
difficulty: easy
topics: [loops, strings]
order: 6
---

Your program is given a whole number `n`. Print its times table from 1 to 10, one line each, in this format:

```
3 x 1 = 3
3 x 2 = 6
...
3 x 10 = 30
```

Use a lowercase letter `x` for "times", with one space on each side of `x` and `=`.
