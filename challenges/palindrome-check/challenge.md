difficulty: medium

A **palindrome** reads the same forwards and backwards, like `racecar`.

Your program is given one line of text. Print `yes` if it's a palindrome, or `no` if it isn't.

When checking, **ignore spaces and capital letters**. So `Never odd or even` counts as a palindrome, because without spaces and in lowercase it's `neveroddoreven`, which reverses to itself. Other characters, like punctuation, are not ignored.
