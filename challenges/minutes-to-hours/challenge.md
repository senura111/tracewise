---
title: Minutes to Hours
difficulty: easy
topics: [numbers, variables]
order: 2
---

Your program is given a number of **minutes** (a whole number, 0 or more). Turn it into hours and minutes.

Print the hours, the letter `h`, a space, then the minutes left over and the letter `m`.

For example, 135 minutes is 2 hours and 15 minutes, so print:

```
2h 15m
```
