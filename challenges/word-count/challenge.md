title: Word Count
difficulty: medium
topics: [dictionaries, strings]
order: 13
---

Your program is given one line of lowercase words separated by spaces. Count how many times each word appears.

Print one line per word, as `word: count`, in the order the words **first** appear.

For example, `the cat and the hat` gives:

```
the: 2
cat: 1
and: 1
hat: 1
```
