Your program is given one line of game scores: whole numbers separated by spaces. There's always at least one score.

Print two lines:

1. `Highest: ` followed by the highest score
2. `Average: ` followed by the average score, **rounded to 1 decimal place**

For example, for `3 9 4` the highest is 9 and the average is 16 ÷ 3 = 5.333…, so print:

```
Highest: 9
Average: 5.3
```

Always show one decimal place, even for a whole number: an average of 10 prints as `10.0`.
