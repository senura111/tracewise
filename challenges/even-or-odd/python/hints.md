A number is even when it can be split into two equal whole halves, so dividing it by 2 leaves nothing over.

---

The `%` operator gives the **remainder** of a division. For example, `7 % 2` is `1` and `8 % 2` is `0`.

---

Use an `if` to check whether `number % 2 == 0`. Print `"even"` when that's true, and use `else` to print `"odd"`.
