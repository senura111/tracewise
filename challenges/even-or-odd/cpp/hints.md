A number is even when dividing it by 2 leaves nothing over.

---

The `%` operator gives the **remainder** of a division: `7 % 2` is `1` and `8 % 2` is `0`.

---

Use `if (number % 2 == 0)` to print `even`, and `else` to print `odd`. Check for `== 0`, not `== 1`: in C++, a negative odd number gives a remainder of `-1`.
