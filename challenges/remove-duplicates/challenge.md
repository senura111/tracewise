---
title: Remove Duplicates
difficulty: medium
topics: [lists, strings]
order: 11
---

Your program is given one line of words separated by spaces. Some words appear more than once.

Print the words with the repeats removed: keep each word the **first** time it appears, in the original order, separated by single spaces.

For example, `apple banana apple cherry banana` becomes:

```
apple banana cherry
```

Words are case-sensitive: `Cat` and `cat` count as different words.
