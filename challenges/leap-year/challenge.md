A **leap year** has an extra day, 29 February. The rules are:

1. A year divisible by **4** is a leap year,
2. **except** a year divisible by **100**, which isn't,
3. **except** a year divisible by **400**, which is.

So 2024 is a leap year, 1900 isn't (divisible by 100), and 2000 is (divisible by 400).

Your program is given a year. Print `<year> is a leap year` or `<year> is not a leap year`. For example:

```
2024 is a leap year
```
